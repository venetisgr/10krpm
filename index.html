What we do

Workshop services

Everything under one roof, from a routine service to a full engine build.

Rotary engine rebuilds

Rebuilds and repairs for 13B, 13B-REW twin turbo and RENESIS engines in the RX-7 and RX-8.

ECU remapping

Custom tuning and engine management with EcuTek, FuelTech, AEM and MoTeC systems.

Servicing & tune-ups

Plugs, leads, coils, filters and oil for MX-5 NB, NC and ND, RX-7 and RX-8, using NGK, K&N and Idemitsu parts.

Ceramic exhaust coating

HVOF zirconia thermal barrier coating for manifolds and exhausts. It cuts exhaust surface temperature by up to a third, so cooler air reaches the intake.

Power exhaust systems

10k RPM manifolds and complete exhaust systems, including Akrapovič.

Transmission

Gearbox and clutch work, Alcon clutches and Idemitsu Racing gear oil.

Brakes & suspension

EBC and Alcon brakes, plus Whiteline and Ultra Racing chassis parts for sharper handling.

Cooling & fuel

Mishimoto radiators and intercoolers, and FuelTech fuel and ignition upgrades.

About 10k RPM

10k RPM is an independent performance workshop in Chalandri, Athens. We look after rotary Mazdas, MX-5s and modified cars, from routine servicing to complete builds.

We work to three words: Power, Torque, Drag. That means quality parts, careful work and clear advice before we touch your car.
